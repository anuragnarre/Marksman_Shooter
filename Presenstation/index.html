Smart Sensor Innovation

Phone Mic Acoustic Muzzle Trigger

Dual Sensor Fusion: Acoustic Timing + Downrange Optical Scoring.

🎤 Zero-Latency Camera Trigger
The phone microphone detects the sharp 5ms acoustic release crack (>75dB) of the air rifle PCP valve, immediately signaling the downrange camera to grab the target impact frame.
⏱️ Built-in Pellet Chronograph ($v_0$)
Measures exact flight time from muzzle click to target entry ($t_{\text{flight}} \approx 57\text{ ms}$). Computes exact muzzle velocity ($v_0 = 175.4\text{ m/s}$) for every shot!
🛡️ 100% False-Positive Immunity
The target camera only records a scored shot when verified by the acoustic muzzle impulse, completely ignoring shadows or camera vibrations.
