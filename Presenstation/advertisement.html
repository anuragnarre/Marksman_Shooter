Dual Sensor Fusion: Acoustic Timing + Optical Scoring

When an athlete squeezes the 10m air rifle trigger, the PCP valve releases with a distinct 5ms acoustic transient crack. The shooter's smartphone mounted at the lane captures this audio impulse instantly:

1. Zero-Latency Camera Wakeup: Instead of continuously streaming 120 FPS video downrange (wasting Wi-Fi bandwidth), the phone mic triggers the downrange camera exactly when the shot is released.
2. Built-In Pellet Flight Chronograph: By measuring the microsecond gap between the muzzle acoustic crack and the target paper optical entry ($t_{\text{flight}} \approx 57\text{ ms}$), Marksman computes exact muzzle velocity ($v_0 = 175.4\text{ m/s}$) for every shot!
3. Zero False Positives: Target camera will never record accidental shadows or flies—only confirmed acoustic shots are plotted on the scoreboard.
